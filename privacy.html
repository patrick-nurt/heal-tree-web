Privacy Policy

The Heal-In Tree · Last updated: November 2026

No Accounts

The Heal In Tree does not require you to create an account. There are no logins, no email addresses, no usernames, and no passwords.

No Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, or any identifying details.

No Stored Messages

When you tap GIVE, a signal is sent through our servers to someone else. When the signal is delivered, it is instantly and permanently deleted. No history of your interactions is kept.

Anonymous Country Detection

To show the golden light trail on the world map, we detect your country from your IP address. This is done at the server level — no location permission is requested from your device. Your IP address is not stored.

No Tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels. We do not track your usage, behavior, or interactions within the app.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties. There is no information to share.

Children

The app contains no content that is inappropriate for any age. There is no user-generated content, no communication between users, and no data collection.
